# ongoing/ — Perpetual Areas of Life

Projects in `projects/` are strictly **finite endeavors** with deliverables and an explicit completion state.

Perpetual areas of life, care, operational upkeep, or ongoing responsibilities that lack termination live here in **`ongoing/`**, not in `projects/`.

## Examples
- `ongoing/health/` — Nutrition, medical records, exercise habits, routines
- `ongoing/home/` — Ongoing household maintenance, appliances, leases
- `ongoing/family/` — Family schedules, school administration, shared logistics
- `ongoing/finances/` — Taxes, investments, recurring bills, budget

Each ongoing area may contain its own `tasks.md`, reference documentation, and guides.
